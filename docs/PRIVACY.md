<a name="english"></a>
## English (US/UK)

### 1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how **Flowonline2** (developed by **PiBOH**, https://github.com/PiBOH/flowonline2) handles information when you use the application.

### 2. Data We Collect

Flowonline2 is a **static, client-side web application**. It runs entirely in your browser:

* **No account, no personal data:** We do not require registration, and we do not collect names, e-mail addresses, or any other personal information.
* **No tracking:** The application does not use analytics, cookies, or fingerprinting.
* **No servers:** All processing (parsing, interpretation, export to SVG/PNG/PDF, code generation) happens locally on your device.

### 3. Local Storage

Flowonline2 may save your flowchart to your browser's **local storage** so your work is not lost on refresh. This data:

* Stays **on your device** — it is never transmitted to us or to any server.
* Can be cleared at any time from the application menu (*Clear Local Storage*), which optionally also clears the current work when the flag is enabled.

### 4. Third-Party Services

* The app is hosted on **GitHub Pages**; the hosting provider may process standard technical logs (IP address, browser, timestamps) in accordance with its own privacy policy.
* The application may fetch documentation (LICENSE, MANUAL.md, CHANGELOG.md) and the version file from **GitHub** (raw.githubusercontent.com) to display up-to-date content.
* The About dialog may show the repository license fetched from GitHub. We do not control GitHub's data handling.

### 5. Your Choices

* Clear your flowchart data at any time with *Clear Local Storage*.
* Use the app in your browser's private/incognito mode to prevent any persistence.

### 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at `docs/PRIVACY.md` in the repository.

### 7. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue or use the repository contact methods at https://github.com/PiBOH/flowonline2.
